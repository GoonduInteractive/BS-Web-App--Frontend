Goondu Games is bringing Beyond Service: Trial of the Gods to ANiWE XCHANGE, and this time players can take it home on their phones. On 5 December 2026 at UN House, Abuja, the Beyond Service mobile beta will be available to download and play for the first time.

Players at the event will be among the first to step into the arena, explore the new content and compete in a live tournament. Our partner studio, Okilo Integrated, will also be bringing Danfo Hustle, its Lagos-themed driving game, so players can take on both games on the day.

“We have spent the last few months refining the player experience based on feedback from our loyal, committed and fun fan base. We can't wait for them to finally have the mobile version on their devices and enjoy Beyond Service daily!” said Sophia Ahuoyiza, Product Engineering Lead at Goondu Games.

### **What's new in Trial of the Gods**

The mobile beta is the biggest step yet for Beyond Service. Players can expect:

* **New characters:** fresh fighters joining the roster, each with their own style and story.
* **New skins:** new looks to customise your characters.
* **Immersive gameplay:** sharper combat and a world built from Igbo mythology, made to pull you in from the first match.

### **Hedera-exclusive skins**

The beta also brings Hedera-exclusive skins, available for the first time at the event. These skins are owned on the Hedera network, so they belong to the player who collects them. Connecting a wallet is optional: the game is open to everyone, and the exclusives are an extra for players who want them.

### **A tournament with over ₦400K in prizes**

The Goondu Games arena at ANiWE XCHANGE includes a live gaming experience and tournament with a prize pool of over ₦400,000, with more than ₦200,000 up for grabs per game.

The tournament runs across both games: Beyond Service: Trial of the Gods from Goondu Games, and Danfo Hustle from Okilo Integrated.

### **More at ANiWE XCHANGE**

ANiWE XCHANGE is more than a tournament. There's something for everyone across the day:

* **Gaming sessions:** open play beyond the tournament.
* **Anime trivia:** put what you know to the test.
* **Cosplay:** come as your favourite character and show it off.
* **Carnival games:** quick, fun games between matches.
* **Panels:** talks and conversations through the day.
* **Free medical checkups:** available on site for attendees.

Whether you're coming to compete, connect, show off your cosplay or just have an unforgettable time, this is where you need to be. The event is supported by UNODC, UNAIDS, UN Women, NACA and APYIN, and Goondu Games is glad to be part of a space where young people can play, connect and see what African studios are building.
