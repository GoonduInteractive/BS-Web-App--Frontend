### **Beyond Service Wins $90,000 at the Hedera Africa Hackathon**

Beyond Service, the African mythology action game from Nigerian studio Goondu Games, is one of the top winners of the 2025 Hedera Africa Hackathon. The Hashgraph Association and Exponential Science announced the winners on 29 April 2026. Beyond Service finished first in its track and fourth across the whole competition.

### **Hedera Africa Hackathon 2025 results for Beyond Service**

* **1st place, Immersive Experiences track:** $50,000
* **4th place, cross-track championship:** $40,000

Together, the two placements brought Goondu Games $90,000. The cross-track championship ranked the strongest projects from every track, so fourth place puts Beyond Service ahead of hundreds of fintech, climate, healthcare and AI projects, not only games.

### **The world's largest Web3 hackathon**

The Hedera Africa Hackathon launched in May 2025 with a $1 million prize pool. By the time winners were announced, it had become the largest Web3 hackathon in the world:

* More than 13,000 developers competed
* Over 1,300 projects were submitted
* More than 45,000 people joined its education and certification programmes across 20+ African hubs and online

The hackathon was co-funded by The Hashgraph Association and Exponential Science in partnership with Orange Digital Center, and operated by Dar Blockchain. Projects competed in four tracks: On-Chain Finance and Real-World Assets, DLT for Operations, Immersive Experiences, and AI and DePIN.

### **About Beyond Service**

Beyond Service is an action game rooted in African mythology, built by Goondu Games in Enugu, Nigeria. Ancestral power drives the fight, and players battle through the Trial in fast, competitive PvP. The game is built mobile-first, because Africa is a mobile-first gaming continent, with PC and console versions to follow.

Beyond Service is developed by the Goondu Games core team: Leslie Osinachi-Okoh (CEO and Lead Game Designer), Goodness Ndukwe (COO and Lead Game Developer), Sophia Ahuoyiza (Product Engineering Lead) and Moses Onwe (UI/UX Designer).

### **Why we built on Hedera**

Players spend hours earning rare items in games, but those items stay locked to the game's servers. Beyond Service entered the hackathon with a different approach: persistent digital identity and player-owned assets.

Hedera mirrors ownership of selected earned items on-chain, so a rare item you win in the Trial is verifiably yours. This adds to the game without gating it. You can play every mode without a crypto wallet, and there are no pay-to-earn mechanics. Hedera is there for players who want ownership on top of the game.

### **What the win means for Goondu Games**

For an independent studio, $90,000 changes what is possible. The prize money is funding development of the Beyond Service mobile game, from core gameplay to the online platform that connects players.

It also puts an African game in front of one of the largest blockchain developer communities in the world, and shows that games built in Nigeria can compete on the global stage.
