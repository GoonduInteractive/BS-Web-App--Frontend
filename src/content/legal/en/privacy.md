  - navLabel: Information We Collect
    heading: INFORMATION WE COLLECT
    body: >-
      Because Beyond Service spans a website, a game, digital and physical
      comics, and a merch store, the categories of data we may collect include:


      a) Information you give us directly

      - Account information: username, email address, password (stored securely,
      never in plain text), date of birth, and optionally a display name or
      avatar.

      - Purchase and payment information: billing name, shipping address, order
      history, and payment confirmation details. Full card numbers are handled
      by our payment processor (see Section 5) — we do not store complete card
      numbers on our own servers.

      - Comics library data: which issues you've purchased, unlocked, or read,
      and your reading progress.

      - Communications: messages you send us via support, email, social media,
      or community channels; survey responses; feedback.

      - User-generated content: anything you post in forums, chat, leaderboards,
      or community features, if and when these exist.


      b) Information collected automatically

      - Device and technical data: IP address, browser type, operating system,
      device identifiers, general location (derived from IP).

      - Usage data: pages visited, features used, session length, in-game
      actions, progression, rank, PvP match history, and similar gameplay
      telemetry.

      - Cookies and similar technologies: see Section 6.


      c) Information from third parties

      - If you sign in or link an account through a third-party platform (e.g.,
      Google, Apple, a console/store account, or a payment provider), we may
      receive basic profile information from that platform, subject to your
      settings with them.


      d) In-game economy data (if applicable)

      - If the Services include virtual currency, items, or a wallet system, we
      collect data related to your balances, transactions, and item history
      within the game.


      We try to collect only what's needed to run Beyond Service well. If a
      specific feature (like the merch store) isn't live yet, we won't collect
      the data tied to it until it is.

    heading: HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
    body: >-
      We use the information above to:


      - Create and manage your account

      - Process purchases, deliver digital comics, and ship physical orders

      - Operate core gameplay: matchmaking, progression, rankings, and
      anti-cheat/anti-fraud systems

      - Sync your comics library and reading progress across devices

      - Provide customer support and respond to your requests

      - Send you service-related communications (order confirmations, security
      alerts, account notices)

      - Send optional marketing or community updates, only where you've opted in
      or where local law allows it

      - Improve and debug the Services, understand how features are used, and
      balance gameplay

      - Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, cheating, abuse, or security
      incidents

      - Comply with legal obligations


      We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their own
      marketing purposes.

    heading: HOW WE SHARE INFORMATION
    body: >-
      We share information only as needed, with:


      - Payment processors (e.g., [Paystack / Flutterwave / Stripe — confirm

      advertising purposes without your consent.
